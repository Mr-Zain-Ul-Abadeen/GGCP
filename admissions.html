Step-by-Step Procedure

The 5-Step Enrollment Roadmap

Follow this official 5-stage procedure to successfully register your application and confirm your enrollment at Govt. Graduate College Pattoki.

01

Online OCAS Portal

Visit the Punjab Online College Admissions System (OCAS) website. Select Govt. Graduate College Pattoki and choose your desired discipline.

02

Bank Challan Payment

Generate the computerized processing fee challan (Rs. 100/-) and deposit it at any authorized branch of The Bank of Punjab (BOP).

03

Document Scrutiny

Submit verified photocopies of your Result Card, CNIC/B-Form, Character Certificate, and photographs to the Campus Admissions Desk.

04

Merit List Verification

Check the notified Open Merit Lists published on the college campus notice boards and official web portal on the scheduled dates.

05

Dues & Roll Number

Deposit the subsidized semester/annual dues at the college fee booth and collect your formal College ID card and Class Section Allotment.

Mandatory Document Scrutiny Rule: Original matriculation or intermediate certificates, character certificates, and character testimonials must be produced in person at the time of final admission clearance. False declarations result in immediate cancellation of candidature.

Applicant Assistance

Admissions FAQ

Answers to common questions regarding eligibility, age relaxation, quotas, and registration steps.

For BS 4-Year degree programs, the merit is calculated as per the Higher Education Department (HED) Punjab formula: 100% aggregate based on Intermediate (HSSC) total marks. Candidates with certified Hafiz-e-Quran credentials receive an additional 20 marks added directly to their obtained aggregate score upon clearing the oral recitation test conducted by the College Quran Committee.

Yes. On the Punjab OCAS portal, candidates can submit applications to multiple departments (e.g. BS Computer Science, BS Physics, and BS Mathematics). A separate processing challan of Rs. 100/- must be deposited for each individual discipline choice. If selected in multiple lists, you may choose your preferred program before fee clearance.

Candidates appearing on the merit list must present: (1) Original Matric and Intermediate Result Cards, (2) Original Character Certificate from the head of the previous institution, (3) Candidate's original CNIC or NADRA B-Form, (4) Father's / Guardian's CNIC photocopy, (5) Four passport-sized photographs with blue background, and (6) No Objection Certificate (NOC) if applying from an education board outside BISE Lahore.

Govt. Graduate College Pattoki operates primarily as a commuter institution serving students across Pattoki, Habibabad, Chunian, Kasur, and surrounding rural tehsils. On-campus residential hostel facilities are limited and allotted exclusively on distance-based merit to BS students residing beyond a 35 km perimeter.

The standard upper age limit is 18 years for Intermediate and 24 years for BS 4-Year programs on the closing date of application submission. Age relaxation up to 2 years can be granted by the College Principal under exceptional circumstances upon written appeal. Relaxation beyond 2 years requires sanction from the Vice Chancellor, University of the Punjab / BISE Lahore Chairman.
