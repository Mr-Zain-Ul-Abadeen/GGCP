About Our College

Our Heritage & Vision

More than Fifty Years of Transformative Education

Founded in 1970, Govt. Graduate College Pattoki was established to democratize access to higher education for students throughout District Kasur. From its modest initial intake, the institution has flourished into a leading public sector graduate college occupying a sprawling 25-acre campus.

Operating under the jurisdiction of the Higher Education Department, Government of the Punjab, and affiliated with the University of the Punjab, Lahore, the college balances scientific exploration with humanist reflection, preparing graduates for competitive national careers and advanced scholarship.
