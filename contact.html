Geographical Orientation

Campus Location & Transit Directions

Conveniently situated along College Road, Pattoki. Accessible via National Highway (N-5) Multan Road and within walking distance from Pattoki Railway Station.

From N-5 National Highway

Take the central Pattoki city bypass exit onto College Road. The college grand gate is located 1.2 km south of the main crossing.

From Pattoki Railway Station

Approximately 8 minutes via local motorized or pedal rickshaws. Continuous transit is available along Station Road directly to the campus.

Subsidized Student Transit

Designated student shuttle vans connect Chunian, Habibabad, and surrounding union councils to the college gate during morning commute hours.
