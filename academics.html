Governance & Policies

Academic Regulations & Student Conduct

Govt. Graduate College Pattoki maintains strict academic discipline in accordance with Higher Education Department Punjab guidelines and University of the Punjab statutes.

  • • Minimum 75% mandatory attendance required in all lectures and laboratory sessions.
  • • Semester GPA grading scale follows standardized PU examination thresholds.
  • • Merit scholarship opportunities available for top percentile students each term.
