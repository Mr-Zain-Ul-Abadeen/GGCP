Our Sports Facilities

Maintained under the Directorate of Physical Education to support regular physical training, departmental leagues, and provincial tournaments.

Natural Turf Oval

Cricket Pavilion & Oval

A manicured 65-meter boundary cricket oval equipped with prepared turf pitches, concrete practice nets, sightscreens, and a shaded spectator pavilion. Hosts annual BISE Lahore inter-collegiate qualifying fixtures.

  • 3 Professional Turf Practice Nets
  • Covered Players Pavilion & Scorers Box
  • Automated Pitch Roller & Irrigation System
Open Daily: 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM & 3:00 PM – 6:30 PM
Standard 105m x 68m

Football Field & Athletics Track

A full-sized regulation natural grass soccer pitch encircled by a standard 400-meter cinder athletic running track, dedicated shot-put circles, and long-jump landing sandpits.

  • Standard 400m Track for 100m–1500m Sprints
  • High Jump, Long Jump & Triple Jump Pits
  • Regulation FIFA-dimension Goalposts
Host of District Kasur Football Tournaments
Indoor Arena

Indoor Gymnasium & Courts

A multi-purpose covered sports hall hosting two wooden badminton courts, international-standard table tennis arenas, judo/taekwondo mats, and strength conditioning weight stations.

  • 2 Hardwood Badminton Courts
  • 4 Double-Fish Official Table Tennis Tables
  • Strength & Conditioning Fitness Center
Climate-Controlled Indoor Playing Area
